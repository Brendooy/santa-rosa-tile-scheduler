SANTA ROSA TILE SCHEDULER  -  USER GUIDE
=========================================

ONE MANAGER AT A TIME
  Each computer keeps its own copy of the data. Changes made on one computer do
  NOT appear on another unless you Export on one and Import on the other.
  Decide which computer is the "master" and do the scheduling there.

THE SCHEDULE TAB
  - Use the arrows to move between weeks (Monday to Saturday).
  - Click a cell and type: 8-5   7:30-4:30   10-3   (times 1-6 mean afternoon)
        7:30        a start time alone means "start to closing"
        OFF         day off
        PTO         paid time off
        IN LATE     comes in later than opening, works to closing
    Press Enter to move down, Tab to move right. Autocomplete suggests common shifts.
  - Cell colours: grey = OFF, yellow = PTO, light blue = IN LATE, red = a problem
    (hover for the reason).
  - "Coverage" rows under each location show: Warehouse and Showroom (enough people
    qualified for each are on the floor all day) and min (fewest people on the floor).
  - Under the grid: "Problems to fix" (these block publishing) and "Warnings".
  - Support row: type names of support staff for each day.

CHECKS (what counts as a problem)
  - A cell is empty or not understood.
  - Someone is scheduled on a day they have PTO, a day they are unavailable, before
    their earliest time or after their latest time, or when the store is closed.
  - Not enough Warehouse or Showroom coverage at some time of the day. One person
    can only fill one position at a time, even if qualified for both.
  - No keyholder at opening or at closing. A keyholder (ticked on the Staff tab) must
    be working when the store opens and when it closes. This check stays off until at
    least one person at that location is marked keyholder.
  Warnings (do not block): fewer people than the store minimum mid-day, a person
  over their max days/hours, MORE THAN 8 PAID HOURS IN ONE DAY for one person,
  SIX DAYS IN A ROW (Monday to Saturday with no day off), a shift well outside store
  hours, PTO marked OFF.

PAID HOURS
  Hours totals take off an unpaid lunch: 30 minutes once a shift is 6 hours or longer,
  and 1 hour once it is 9 hours or longer. So 8-4 = 7.5 paid hours, 8-5 = 8, 7:30-5 = 8.5.
  The lunch lengths and the "more than 8 hours" limit can be changed on the Store rules tab.

AUTO-FILL
  Fills the week from each person's availability, usual shift, target days per
  week, qualifications and PTO, then repairs keyholder/Warehouse/Showroom/staffing gaps. It tries not to go over
  anyone's max hours per week (set on the Staff tab) or 8 paid hours in a day, by
  shortening shifts or giving a day off where coverage allows, and avoids 6-day weeks;
  anything it cannot avoid is reported.
  If the week already has entries you choose: fill empty cells only, or rebuild.
  It tells you about anything it could not cover. Always review the result;
  every cell can still be edited. "Copy last week" copies the previous week.

DRAFT AND FINAL
  New weeks are DRAFT. "Publish as final" is allowed only when there are no
  problems (warnings need a confirmation). A final week is locked; "Reopen as
  draft" unlocks it. Publishing does not send anything anywhere: use Print or
  Save PDF to give the schedule to employees. Drafts print with a DRAFT mark.

STAFF TAB
  Name, location, active, can work Warehouse / Showroom, keyholder, target and max days per week,
  max hours, and per-day availability (available, earliest start, latest end,
  usual shift, prefer-off). Untick "Active" to hide someone without losing history.

STORE RULES TAB
  Opening/closing times for each day, default shifts, how many people must be on
  the floor, how many Warehouse and Showroom people are required, unpaid lunch, and
  the printed title.

TIME OFF & GOOGLE TAB
  - Time off can be typed in here (works offline) and/or imported from a Google
    Calendar. Imported time off is stored on this computer and used offline.
  - Connect: click "Connect Google Calendar"; your browser opens; sign in and allow
    READ-ONLY access. Choose the calendar(s) that hold time off. Then "Refresh time
    off now". Needs internet.
  - An ALL-DAY calendar event whose title contains an employee's name (for example
    "Brendan off" or "Haley vacation") becomes time off for those dates.
    If the title says "works" (for example "Brendan Works") it is an EXTRA WORK DAY,
    not time off: that day's availability is ignored, Auto-fill schedules them, and
    you get a warning if they are marked OFF. Timed events (appointments) are ignored. Events that match nobody are listed so you
    can fix a spelling.
  - The page and the Schedule tab show when time off was last refreshed. If a
    refresh fails, your existing time off is kept and a red banner says so.
    Time off added in Google since the last refresh will NOT show until you refresh.

DATA & BACKUPS TAB
  - Export All Data / Import Data: move everything to another computer. Import
    shows what will be replaced, asks you to confirm, and backs up your current
    data first.
  - Backups are made automatically when the app starts and closes and every few
    hours if something changed (newest 30 kept). "Restore..." brings one back
    (after backing up the current data).
  - If the data file is ever damaged, the app loads the newest backup and tells you.

PRINTING
  Print opens the Windows print dialog (landscape, one page). Save PDF writes a
  PDF file you can email or text.

SAMPLE DATA
  On first start the app contains the staff and the week of 26 October from the
  posted schedule. Warehouse/Showroom qualifications (everyone starts with both), store hours, minimum
  staffing and target days were GUESSED from that photo. Please review them on the
  Staff and Store rules tabs before relying on Auto-fill.
