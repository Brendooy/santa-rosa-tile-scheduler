GOOGLE CALENDAR SETUP  (one time, by whoever prepares the app)
=============================================================

The app reads PTO from Google Calendar with READ-ONLY access, signing in through the
manager's own web browser. For that, Google needs to know about the app. You create
that registration once; managers then just click "Connect Google Calendar".

Without this the app still works fully; only the Google import is unavailable
(time off can be typed in on the Time off & Google tab).

A. CREATE THE GOOGLE REGISTRATION (about 10 minutes)
   1. Go to https://console.cloud.google.com and sign in with the company Google
      account. Create a project named "Santa Rosa Tile Scheduler".
   2. Menu > APIs & Services > Library > search "Google Calendar API" > Enable.
   3. Menu > APIs & Services > OAuth consent screen (or "Google Auth Platform").
        - If the company uses Google Workspace: choose user type INTERNAL.
          (Best choice: no Google review, no warning screens, no 7-day expiry.)
        - Otherwise choose EXTERNAL, fill in app name and your email, and when
          finished click "Publish app" so the status is "In production".
          If you leave it in "Testing", sign-ins EXPIRE AFTER 7 DAYS and managers
          must reconnect weekly. In production but unverified, Google shows a
          "Google hasn't verified this app" screen the first time; the manager
          clicks Advanced > "Go to Santa Rosa Tile Scheduler (unsafe)". This is
          expected for a private tool.
   4. Data access / Scopes > Add:  .../auth/calendar.readonly   (read-only).
   5. APIs & Services > Credentials > Create credentials > OAuth client ID.
        Application type: DESKTOP APP.  Name: Santa Rosa Tile Scheduler.
      Click "Download JSON".

B. GIVE THE FILE TO THE APP  (pick one)
   - Easiest for managers: rename the downloaded file to   google-client.json
     and put it in the app folder next to "Santa Rosa Tile Scheduler.exe" BEFORE
     zipping and sharing. Managers then only click Connect.
   - Or: managers open the Time off & Google tab and paste the Client ID (and
     Client secret) when asked.
   (For a Desktop-app client Google says the secret is not truly confidential,
    so shipping it in the folder is the normal practice. It is NOT included in
    data exports. A manager's actual sign-in is separate; see below.)

C. WHAT MANAGERS DO
   1. Time off & Google tab > "Connect Google Calendar". The browser opens.
   2. Sign in to the Google account that can see the time-off calendar, click
      Allow (read-only calendar access). The browser says "Connected".
   3. Back in the app, tick the calendar(s) that contain time off, and
      click "Refresh time off now".
   4. Name events like "Brendan - PTO" as ALL-DAY events. The employee's name in
      the Staff tab must appear in the title (first name is enough).

GOOGLE SHEETS: SEND PUBLISHED WEEKS TO A SHARED SHEET  (optional, one time + once per computer)
=====================================================================================
When a week is published as final, the app can copy it into ONE shared Google Sheet:
a new tab "Week of YYYY-MM-DD" per week; older weeks' tabs stay; republishing a week
overwrites that week's tab. This is a SECOND, separate Google sign-in. The calendar
sign-in above stays read-only and is not changed.

WHAT THE PERMISSION MEANS: Google calls it "See, edit, create and delete all your
Google Sheets spreadsheets". While connected, the app can view and edit your Google
Sheets. The app only writes to the one spreadsheet whose link you give it, only adds/
overwrites its own "Week of ..." tabs, and never touches any other tab. (It also learns
the email address of the account that connected, to show it and to name it in messages.)

ONE-TIME SETUP IN GOOGLE CLOUD (same project as above):
   1. APIs & Services > Library > search "Google Sheets API" > Enable.
   2. OAuth consent screen / Google Auth Platform > Data access (Scopes) > Add scopes:
        .../auth/spreadsheets      (and keep .../auth/calendar.readonly)
      "openid" and "email" are added automatically and need no review.
   3. If the app is External and "In production" nothing else is needed. If it is still
      in "Testing", add the Google account(s) that will connect under Test users
      (and see the 7-day warning below).
   4. Re-consent: Google only grants the new permission when each person clicks
      "Connect Google Sheets" in the app and allows it. Existing calendar sign-ins do
      not include it.

SHARE THE SHEET: open the Google Sheet > Share > add the Google account that will click
"Connect Google Sheets" as an EDITOR. (Without Editor access the app says "The connected
Google account can't edit this Sheet. Share it with <email> as Editor.")

IN THE APP (each computer, once):
   Time off & Google tab > "Google Sheet for published schedules":
   1. Click "Connect Google Sheets", sign in with the account that is an Editor, Allow.
   2. Paste the Google Sheet link into "Google Sheet link".
   3. Click "Test connection": it shows the spreadsheet's title and who is connected.
   4. Leave "Send to Google Sheet when I publish" ticked. Publishing a week now sends it.
   Schedule tab: "Send this week to Google Sheet" re-sends a published week by hand; the
   line next to it says "Sent to Google Sheet 3:04 PM" or "Not sent: <reason>" in red.
   If a send fails (offline, Google busy) the week stays published and is retried the next
   time the app starts, or when you click the button. Reopening a week as a draft does not
   change the Sheet: its tab keeps the last published version.

The Sheets sign-in token is stored encrypted as google-sheets-auth.bin in the data folder.
It is never exported. Disconnect it with the Disconnect button in that card, or at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

SECURITY NOTES
  - The sign-in token is stored encrypted with Windows (tied to the Windows user
    account) in the data folder as google-auth.bin. It is never part of "Export
    All Data". Moving to another computer = connect again there.
  - To remove access: Time off & Google > Disconnect, or revoke it at
    https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
  - The app only ever READS the calendar. It cannot create or change events.

TROUBLESHOOTING
  "Google hasn't verified this app"  Expected for external, unverified apps (see A3).
  "Access was revoked or expired"    Click "Reconnect" (the app shows a Reconnect button
                                     for Calendar and for Sheets). Google expires sign-ins
                                     after about 7 days while the consent screen is in
                                     "Testing"; publish the app to stop this (step A3).
  "Calendar API may not be enabled"  Repeat step A2 in the same project.
  "Google Sheets API is not turned on"  Enable the Google Sheets API (Sheets setup, step 1).
  "Sheet not found. Check the link." Paste the full link of the spreadsheet again.
  Nothing imports                    Check the event is ALL-DAY and the title includes
                                     the employee's name; see "not matched" list.
